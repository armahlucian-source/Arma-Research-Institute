01 / Research Agenda

Divisions & Focus

Classical compartmental models assume homogeneous mixing—treating biological tissues, biofilms, and communities as well-mixed fluids. Our work replaces mean-field assumptions with explicit spatial geometry and reproducible discrete-time simulations.

DIV. 01

Computational Biology

2D Moore-neighborhood SIR cellular automata modeling local pathogen propagation, self-shading recovered shells, and peak prevalence suppression.

N = 10,000 cells · Seed 3 × 3
DIV. 02

Percolation & Scaling

Finite-size scaling across L = 50, 100, and 200 lattices to locate dynamical percolation transitions (vc ≈ 55–57%) relative to static site percolation (vc ≈ 59.3%).

P(S → I) = 1 − (1 − β)k
DIV. 03

Open Scientific Computing

Deterministic NumPy SeedSequence pipelines executed to full extinction (up to 2,500 steps), eliminating finite-horizon truncation artifacts.

05 / The Institute

Independent Science from Accra

Founded in Accra without external grant restrictions, Arma Research Institute publishes open-access computational models, full replication datasets, and lattice simulation software for spatial epidemiology and complex systems.

Every manuscript and Python/NumPy simulation pipeline is embedded directly on this platform for immediate inspection, download, and independent verification.
